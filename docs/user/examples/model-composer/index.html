Model Composer

Device models built from data: a GaN FET from synthetic DC, pulsed and two-temperature S data, with a fitted network, ANN currents and charges, self-heating and drain lag, placed as an 8-finger device in a power sweep and a loadpull; a spiral-inductor family placed between two measured turn counts; and a model from a simulator's tables.

Open this example

File ▸ New ▸ Examples ▸ Model Composer copies this workspace to a folder you choose and opens it on this README. Every example is listed in Example Workspaces.

Three device models built from data with Tools ▸ Model Composer…, and the circuits that use them. Every data file here is synthetic: circuitRF simulated it from a device whose equations are known, and each data set's note says so. That is what makes the example checkable. The model can be compared with the device it was built from, which measured data never allows.

Open a .cmc from the project tree to see its steps, its plots and its results.

synthetic-gan

A GaN FET, four fingers of 125 µm, measured as a lab would measure one:

Data set What it is
dc_25C DC I–V at 25 °C, Vgs −5 … 1.4 V, Vds 0 … 20 V. The junction heats, and drain lag sits at its DC state.
pulsed_q0_25C, pulsed_q0_125C Pulsed I–V from a quiescent point of (−6 V, 0 V), at two chuck temperatures.
pulsed_q10_25C, pulsed_q20_25C Pulsed I–V from quiescent drain voltages of 10 V and 20 V.
pulsed_dp20_25C A double pulse: a 50 µs pre-pulse to 20 V sets the traps, then the measuring pulse 1 µs later.
s_25C, s_85C S-parameters at 49 biases and two temperatures, 0.5 … 20 GHz.

The data is at the probe plane. The pads come off with the open and short dummies in pads/, and the network is the GaNTwoShell cell, the stock GaN two-shell network placed in this workspace as a cell of its own.

The document carries every step:

  • Network: Lg, Ld and Ls fitted by the multi-bias method; every other value is held at the stock value.
  • Thermal: self-heating with Rth = 25 °C/W and τth = 10 µs, typed rather than extracted (see the note below).
  • Current: ANN Id and Ig, with the junction temperature and the drain-lag state as inputs.
  • Traps: CaptureEmission drain lag at its defaults, from the three trap-setting levels 0, 10 and 20 V. The pulses from 10 V that go above 10 V lie beyond their trap-setting bias and are kept for validation only.
  • Charge: adjoint-trained ANN charges from both S sets.

The exported model is gan_4x125.cmodel. Two test benches place it as an 8-finger device (N=8), so its core is doubled, its series resistances halved and its Rth halved:

Bench What it runs
GanPowerSweep A class-AB stage at 2 GHz, Vgs −0.8 V, Vdd 8 V, 50 Ω in and out, drive swept −4 … 22 dBm
GanLoadpull The same device over 49 loads centred between its maximum-power and maximum-efficiency loads, each driven to 3 dB compression
GanVerilogA The same device twice on identical bias networks: as the .cmodel on ports 1 and 2, and as gan_4x125.va on ports 3 and 4. A Vgs sweep, S-parameters and a drive sweep 0 … 16 dBm, each measure in pairs. Needs a Verilog-A compiler

gan_4x125.va is the same model as Verilog-A source, for another simulator: Export Verilog-A… on the Export step, or circuitrf model export synthetic-gan/gan_4x125.cmodel -o synthetic-gan/gan_4x125.va.

Settings chosen for this example, with what a real job would use:

Setting Here A real job
Data points ≤ 150 per I–V set, 49 S biases, 12 frequencies Denser near the knee and pinch-off; 50+ frequencies
Rth Typed, 25 °C/W Extracted from pulsed I–V at two or more chuck temperatures, when drain lag is not modelled; typed from a thermal simulation when it is
IgFloor 1e-7 The default 1e-4 suits a gate whose leakage is not needed. This example's gate current spans ten decades, and at pinch-off the default's resolution is microamps
Network Three inductances free Every value free that the data determines; check the ± and the ⚠ marks
Drain bias 8 V. The drain swings to about twice its bias, and this data ends at Vds 20 V The rated drain voltage, with I–V data reaching past twice it

Why Rth is typed. A pulse from a 0 V drain leaves the drain-lag state half a volt short of its DC value, because capture slows as the gap closes. Rth extraction compares pulsed and DC currents at the same bias, and reads that half volt as heating. With drain lag modelled, type Rth.

The data reaches −5 V and +1.4 V on the gate on purpose. In the power sweep the gate swings from about −4.4 V into forward conduction. A model is continued linearly outside its data, with an added conductance, and a gate that swings past the last measured Vgs is no longer the device.

passive-spiral

Six spiral inductors of 1.5 … 6.5 turns, as two-port S-parameters from 0.2 to 20 GHz. spiral.cmc fits the library's spiral topology to all six at once, with one law per element value in the turns. Its substrate network under port 2 is tied to the one under port 1, and the 3.5-turn spiral is held out and predicted. It exports the cell SpiralFamily, whose one parameter is turns. The element values are variables on the cell's own schematic, each a law of turns written in nH, fF and Ω: x is turns normalised to the fitted range, and Ls, Cs, Cox1 and the rest are polynomials in x.

SpiralFilter places that cell at 3 turns, between two measured spirals, in a π low-pass between 50 Ω ports. Set turns outside 1.5 … 6.5 and the run warns once.

simulated-tables

tables.npy is what a simulator writes for a transistor: per bias, the terminal voltages and currents, the intrinsic voltages its internal nodes carry, and Y at 1 GHz. fet_tables.cmc imports it at the device plane. Because the tables also carry intrinsic voltages, de-embedding checks its own computed ones against them, and the Data step reports the largest difference. The network is held at the stock values the tables were simulated with. The data is at one temperature, so the model has no temperature dependence.

Every step above is a circuitrf model noun:

circuitrf model deembed synthetic-gan/gan_4x125.cmc
circuitrf model fit synthetic-gan/gan_4x125.cmc --step all
circuitrf model validate synthetic-gan/gan_4x125.cmc
circuitrf model export synthetic-gan/gan_4x125.cmc -o synthetic-gan/gan_4x125.cmodel
circuitrf model export synthetic-gan/gan_4x125.cmodel -o synthetic-gan/gan_4x125.va
