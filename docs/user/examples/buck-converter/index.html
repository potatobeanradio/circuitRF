Buck Converter

A 12 V to 5.5 V buck at 1 MHz with a voltage-controlled switch, a diode and an LC filter: start-up from rest, ripple and settling, and initial conditions (IC=) that start the run in steady state.

A 12 V to 5.5 V buck converter switching at 1 MHz, simulated in time: the start-up from rest, the output ripple and how long the output takes to settle, and how initial conditions skip the start-up when only the steady state is wanted.

Run Startup first. SteadyState's initial conditions are numbers read from its result.

The circuit

VIN (12 V) feeds the voltage-controlled switch S1, whose control is VG, a 5 V gate drive at Fsw = 1 MHz with duty Duty = 0.5 (both are variables on the VAR block). S1 is 0.1 Ω on and 1 MΩ off, turning on above 3 V of control and off below 2 V, with a smooth transition between. When it opens, the inductor current carries on through the free-wheeling diode D1. L1 (10 µH) and C1 (1 µF) filter the switched node into a 5 Ω load. The IProbe IL records the inductor current.

The load RL is a Term of 5 Ω. Outside S-parameters a Term is a load of its Z, so here it is a 5 Ω resistor.

Startup

InitialState=zero: every voltage and current starts at 0, the supply included, so the run begins with the supply switched on. 200 µs is about ten periods of the LC filter's 50 kHz resonance, which a 5 Ω load damps to a Q of 1.6.

Measure Result What it is
Vout 5.518 V V(out) averaged over the last switching cycle
Ripple 39.9 mV Peak to peak over the last switching cycle
Overshoot_pct 34.4 % How far the output rises past 5.5 V on the way up
Tsettle 34.9 µs When the output stays within 2 % of its final value
IL_valley 0.947 A The inductor current at its lowest in the last cycle

The output is below Duty × 12 V = 6 V by the diode's drop during the off half of each cycle and the switch's resistance.

SteadyState

The same circuit, started where Startup ends. A switching cycle begins when S1 turns on, which is when the inductor current is at its valley, so the run takes two initial conditions from Startup:

  • IC="out=5.518" on the analysis card holds the output at 5.518 V while the operating point is solved and releases it at t = 0. It is V(out) at the start of a cycle late in Startup.
  • IC=0.947 on L1 starts the inductor at 0.947 A, the IL_valley above.

The output is steady from the first cycle, so 10 µs replaces 200 µs:

Measure Startup (last cycle of 200 µs) SteadyState (last cycle of 10 µs)
Vout 5.5175 V 5.5172 V
Ripple 39.85 mV 39.95 mV
IL_valley 0.9470 A 0.9471 A

Without the inductor's IC the output still starts at 5.518 V, but the inductor starts empty and the filter rings again: the output swings 1.95 V over the first 10 µs and the last cycle reads 5.19 V. Both stored energies have to be right.

Things to try

  • Change Duty to 0.4 and run Startup: the output settles at 4.26 V with 38 mV of ripple, and IL_valley is 0.70 A. SteadyState's IC values then need reading again.
  • circuitrf tran "Buck Converter/Startup/schematic/Startup.csch" runs the same bench headlessly and prints the measures.

Settings traded for run time

None. Step=10n is the output grid, 100 points per switching cycle, and the largest step. Inside it the engine lands a step on every corner of VG and takes at least eight steps across each turn-on and turn-off of S1 (its TransitionPoints), so the edges are resolved whatever the grid.
